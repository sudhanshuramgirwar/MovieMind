HOW TO READ THESE NUMBERS

A transparent baseline

Ratings are split into training and test sets using a fixed random seed. A rating of 4 or higher counts as relevant. Recommendations use the training users' strongest genres (or a popularity fallback), while rating estimates use the training mean for each movie. This simple baseline is easy to explain and provides a starting point for future improvements.
